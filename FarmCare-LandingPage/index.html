COMPREHENSIVE FARM CARE

Essential Care Pillars for Your Dairy Herd

Simple, science-backed guidance to optimize animal health, comfort, and milk quality.

Nutrition & Feed
🌱

Feeding & Daily Nutrition

Ensure well-balanced energy, protein, and minerals tailored to lactating and dry animals.

  • Green & Dry Fodder: 70:30 ideal roughage ratio for rumen digestion
  • Clean Water: 60-90 liters of fresh, cool water accessible 24/7
  • Mineral Mixture: 50g daily salt and chelated mineral supplement
Daily Routine Nutrition Guide →
Shelter & Comfort
🐄

Cow & Buffalo Housing

Stress-free barn environments promote rumination, better immunity, and higher milk yields.

  • Bedding & Dry Floor: Clean dry straw or rubber mats to prevent foot rot
  • Ventilation & Shade: Ample cross-breeze and roof insulation for heat control
  • Gentle Handling: Zero stress, calm movement, and spacious resting cubicles
Housing Standards Barn Layouts →
Veterinary Care
🩺

Preventive Health & Vet Care

Timely vaccinations, deworming, and early sickness detection protect entire herds.

  • Vaccination Calendar: Timely FMD, HS, BQ, and Brucellosis immunization
  • Deworming Schedule: Biannual deworming before and after rainy seasons
  • Early Symptom Alert: Monitor rumination rate, body temperature, and eye clarity

Calf Raising
🍼

Calf Health & Growth

The future of your dairy herd starts with proper neonatal nourishment and maternal care.

  • Colostrum Feeding: 2-3 liters within 2 hours of birth for immunity antibodies
  • Navel Hygiene: 7% tincture iodine application to prevent joint infections
  • Calf Starter: High-protein grain introduction from 2nd week onward
Early Care Calf Handbook →
Milk Hygiene
🥛

Clean Milking & Udder Care

Prevent mastitis, maintain herd longevity, and produce clean, premium-grade milk.

  • Pre & Post Teat Dip: Disinfect teats with food-grade sanitizing dip
  • Dry & Clean Towels: Single-use cloth or paper for individual cows
  • Consistent Timings: Maintain strict 12-hour intervals for cow calmness
Hygiene Protocols Milking Checklist →
☀️

Seasonal Weather Management

Protect dairy animals from severe summer heat stress, monsoon dampness, and winter cold drafts.

  • Summer Cooling: Water sprinklers, fans, and roof thatch to lower temp
  • Monsoon Care: Dry lime powder on barn floor to stop hoof infections
  • Winter Protection: Night curtains and warm bedding for calves
💡 Pro Farmer Tip:

Cows drinking chilled clean water in summer can boost feed intake by up to 18%.

FARMER'S DAILY PLAYBOOK

The 3-Step Daily Care Routine

Easy daily habits practiced by top dairy farmers for zero-disease herds.

05:00 AM - 08:00 AM

Morning Routine

  • ✓ Clean water troughs & refill with fresh water
  • ✓ Check cud chewing (rumination) while cows are resting
  • ✓ Sanitize udders and perform pre-milking inspection
  • ✓ Feed morning green chop + concentrate balance
12:00 PM - 02:00 PM

Midday Check

  • ✓ Activate misting fans during hot afternoon hours
  • ✓ Inspect dung consistency & appetite of all animals
  • ✓ Clean barn dung gutters to maintain low fly activity
  • ✓ Top up mineral lick blocks and electrolyte water
05:00 PM - 08:00 PM

Evening & Night Routine

  • ✓ Evening milking with complete post-milking teat dip
  • ✓ Evening dry fodder and grain feed allocation
  • ✓ Replenish fresh dry straw bedding for comfortable sleep
  • ✓ Lock barn gates and ensure gentle night ventilation
